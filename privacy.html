Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect: nothing

This website collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cookies set by this site.

The contact form sends nothing

The contact form on this site only checks your message in your browser. It does not send, save, or store anything. To actually reach Syed Enterprises, email contact@syedsenterprises.org — that email is the only personal data involved, and only if you choose to write to us.

Fonts

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches them, Google may see your IP address. See Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

External links

Links to our ventures (LookForIt, Estate Path, RepRoaster, GitHub, YouTube and others) lead to third-party websites with their own privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

Your rights (India — DPDP Act 2023)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask what personal data of yours we hold, and ask us to correct or delete it. The answer for this site is simple: we hold none, because nothing here collects it. If you emailed us and want that email deleted, write to contact@syedsenterprises.org and we will delete it.

Children

We collect no personal data from anyone, including anyone under 18, because there is nothing on this site that collects data.

Contact

For any question about this policy, write to contact@syedsenterprises.org.
