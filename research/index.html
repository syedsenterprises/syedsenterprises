Artificial Intelligence

Graph-First, Reproduce-First: A Small-Model Coding Agent Design for Gemma 4 E4B Under a 12-Hour Budget

A coding agent for Google's Gemma 4 E4B, built around graph-first localisation, reproduce-first patching and hard per-task caps. Tested on 129 real tasks: 12 solved (9.3%) in 3h 34m. The paper reports the failures as carefully as the wins. Submitted to the Paper Track of the Gemma 4 Developer Agent Competition.

Statistics · Environment

Seasonal Variation and a Declining Trend in Hyderabad's Air Quality, 2015–2020: A Statistical Analysis of Daily CPCB AQI Data

Using 2,006 days of CPCB AQI data, Hyderabad's air improved significantly: mean AQI fell from 143.42 in 2015 to 93.98 in 2019, a real decline of 13.1 points per year — not a lockdown artefact. Winter is worst (131.24), monsoon best (85.52). PM2.5 is the main driver. But mean PM2.5 is still ~9× the WHO guideline.
